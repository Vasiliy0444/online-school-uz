#### Conditions for receiving Cashback
The IELTS Cashback bonus is available to students who complete the IELTS course at INTER NATION SCHOOL, attend at least 24 IELTS lessons (without interruptions), and achieve an IELTS score of 7.5 or higher within 180 days after completing the course. If a student achieves an official IELTS score of 7.5 or above, they will receive the following cashback: IELTS 7.5 - 1 000 000 UZS IELTS 8.0 - 2 000 000 UZS IELTS 8.5 - 10 000 000 UZS IELTS 9.0 - 100 000 000 UZS - To receive the cashback, the student must agree to participate in a promotional video for the educational center's social media channels. If the student refuses to participate in the promotional video, INTER NATION reserves the right not to award the bonus. - The student must allow the publication of a photo of their original IELTS certificate (with a score of 7.5 or higher). - Students who receive the cashback are not allowed to publish or promote their IELTS results on the social media platforms or advertisements of other English language learning centers. Otherwise, the bonus will be fully canceled. - The bonus will be paid within 90 days after the student submits their IELTS certificate. - All bonuses may be paid in cash, via bank transfer, or through another monetary payment method. - The IELTS result bonus is awarded only once. Even if the student later achieves a higher score, no additional bonus will be granted.
